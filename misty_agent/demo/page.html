Misty LLM Agent

A language model decides what this robot does: when to speak, when to come closer, and when to leave someone alone. It acts one tool call at a time, and an attention loop in front of it decides whether an interaction should begin at all.

Every example below is a recording of one of those runs, made in simulation. Pick one, press play, and step through what Misty perceived and what it chose to do about it. Nothing on this page needs a robot or an API key.
